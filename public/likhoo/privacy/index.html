Privacy Policy

Likhoo — Learn to Write (com.vexoradevelopers.likhoo)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Likhoo is a handwriting and reading app for young children. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and why.

The short version. Likhoo does not ask for, collect or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no chat, no location, no camera, no microphone and no contacts access. Your child's progress stays on the phone. The app shows a small number of child-safe adverts from Google AdMob between lessons, never during one.

Who is responsible

Likhoo is published by Vexora Developers & Consulting Private Limited, Khairahani-01, Chitwan, Nepal. If you have a question about this policy, write to vexoradevelopers@gmail.com.

What stays on the device

Which letters and words your child has finished, the app language, the sound and music settings, the screen direction and the optional daily play time are saved only on the phone or tablet. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Advertising

Likhoo is free and is supported by adverts from Google AdMob. Because Likhoo is made for children:

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children and as coming from a user under the age of consent (COPPA and GDPR child treatment).
  • Adverts are limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • Adverts are not personalised, and the app does not use the Android advertising ID. The AD_ID permission is removed from the app.
  • An advert can only appear after a finished letter or word, when the child taps Next, and at most once every few minutes. Never when the app opens and never during a lesson.

To show adverts, count them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the internet (IP) address, the device type, the Android version and how an advert was viewed. Google handles this under its own policies: see the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob treats child-directed apps.

What Likhoo does not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or photos.
  • Location. The app has no location permission.
  • Recordings. The app has no microphone or camera permission. The voices in the app are recordings that ship with it.
  • Payment information. There is nothing to buy in Likhoo.

Parents stay in control

Settings, this policy and the credits sit behind a parent gate (a question for grown-ups). From the Parent Zone you can change the language, switch music or sound off, choose the screen direction and set an optional daily play time.

Children's privacy

Likhoo is designed to follow the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and GDPR-K (European Union and United Kingdom). Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe information about your child has been shared, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and inside the app under Parent Zone > Privacy policy.
